# E10 Markdown extraction comparison — 2026-09-06

Nine frozen Markdown files; fixed E5 `1200/0`; twelve answered queries and three no-answer probes. Raw and normalized extraction both achieved rank 1 on 12/12 answered queries. Advisory pre-tokenizer passage checks improved 6/12 to 8/12, total chunks fell 3,093 to 2,973, and indexing wall time remained about 666–667 seconds per arm. Raw remains the default.
